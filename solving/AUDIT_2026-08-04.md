## Phase-6 literature notes (primary sources)

- McLeish et al. 2024, Abacus embeddings (arXiv:2405.17399): +input injection +looped → 99% @
  100-digit addition (6×). Long-operand setting; our abacus port failed on 4-digit spans — copy the
  *input-recall* ingredient, retest locally (hosted input-inject negative was STE/budget-confounded).
- Cho et al. 2024, Position Coupling (arXiv:2405.20671) + ICLR'25 multi-level coupling+scratchpad:
  first real multiplication length-gen. Suggests coupling N-digits to remainder-digits by significance.
- Fan et al., Looped Transformers for Length Generalization (ICLR 2025): loops = known step count,
  no intermediate supervision → strong extrapolation. Suggests loops = T training (T is in the prompt — legal).
- "When Does Recurrence Become an Algorithm?" (arXiv:2607.20594): tying selects serial algorithms;
  curriculum dissolves optimization walls; warm-starting transfers mechanisms and beats seed
  lotteries. Suggests operator-first curriculum + warm-start for Task B recurrent cards.
- "Stability and Generalization in Looped Transformers" (arXiv:2604.15259): input recall + outer
  norm are the necessary ingredients — our refuted workspace lacked a recall ablation.
- "Why Can't Transformers Learn Multiplication?" (arXiv:2510.00184): running-partial-sum via tiny
  linear probe → exact 4×4 multiplication. Independent replication of our Task A finding; motivates
  probe-style aux over output-sequence traces (consistent with our Task D null).
- Africa et al., Learning Modular Exponentiation (arXiv:2506.23679): reciprocal operand sampling →
  large gains, synchronized grokking across *families of related moduli*. Basis of v2; also suggests
  structured multi-N families over arbitrary pairs for the N-ladder.
