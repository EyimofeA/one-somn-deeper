UT Depth Embeddings: The Architectural Win

UT (Universal Transformer with depth embeddings) vs plain tied loops. The one architectural change that consistently beats baseline across all splits.

What this shows

Split UT Score Plain Score Delta
e1 K=2 6.50% 6.20% +0.30
e5 K=4 1.00% 0.80% +0.20

Adding a learned depth embedding per loop iteration (tells the model “you’re on loop 3 of 4”) costs ~100 params and consistently improves generalization. One of the few changes that’s never regressed.

Token Cost Reality

8.29M tokens sounds high, but 75.7% are cached (system prompt, AGENTS.md, skill descriptions — identical every turn). Those cost near-zero. The actual paid tokens are ~2M, and at $0.21/M blended, that’s about $0.42 in actual compute cost. The rest of the $1.70 is output tokens (model responses, thinking blocks).

Bottom line: deepseek-v4-pro with caching = dirt cheap. Your $60 budget is effectively unlimited for pi usage.
